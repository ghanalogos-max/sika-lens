FAQ

Common questions

Short answers about privacy, MoMo balances, offline use, and store billing.

Does SikaLens upload my MoMo or bank data?

No. Your wallets, transactions, budgets, and PIN stay on your phone. SikaLens does not create an account and does not upload your ledger to our servers.

Which MoMo networks does it support?

Paste SMS from MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money. You can also track bank and cash wallets with opening balances you set.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The ledger is offline-first on your device. You only need a connection for store downloads, optional currency rates, or when you choose to share an export or backup.

Why doesn’t my wallet match my real MoMo balance?

Set the opening balance, then paste or record every send, receive, cash-out, airtime, bill, and fee for that wallet. Skipping a message leaves the on-phone total behind your real balance.

Is Premium required?

No. Core tracking works without Premium. Optional Premium will bill through Google Play or the App Store when store billing is live; until then purchase shows Coming soon and is not charged.

How do backups and exports work?

You create an encrypted backup file with a passphrase you choose, then share it yourself. CSV and PDF exports are files you save or share; raw MoMo SMS text is omitted from CSV.

When will it be on Google Play and the App Store?

Listings are preparing. The download buttons already point at the final package URLs. Email support@sikalens.app if you want an update when they go live.
