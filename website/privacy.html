Privacy

Last updated for SikaLens by Celestial Web Solutions.

On your phone

Your wallets, transactions, budgets, and PIN stay on this device. SikaLens does not create an account and does not upload your ledger to our servers.

Backups you choose

An encrypted backup is a file you create and share yourself. The passphrase is never stored by SikaLens. The PIN is a one-way hash in the phone’s secure storage and is not inside the backup.

Exports

CSV and PDF exports are files you save or share. They include the transactions you choose to export. Raw MoMo SMS text is never included in the CSV.

Store purchases

Premium is billed by Google Play or the App Store. Those stores handle payment details under their own privacy policies. Celestial Web Solutions does not receive your card number.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@sikalens.app
