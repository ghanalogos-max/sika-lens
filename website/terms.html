What the app is for

SikaLens helps you track MoMo and bank money on your phone. It is not a bank, not a payment service, and not financial advice. Network and bank names and logos belong to their owners.

MoMo and bank balances

Each wallet or bank account keeps an opening balance you set. Every recorded entry then adds or subtracts from that figure. Balances do not restart from zero when you add transactions.

For MoMo amounts to stay correct, paste or record every MoMo transaction for that wallet. If you skip a message, the wallet on this phone will not match your real MoMo balance.
